There’s a reason poets have called Kashmir paradise for centuries: the valley reinvents itself every few months. At Khwab Villa in Preng, Kangan, we see all four seasons play out across the orchard, the lawn and the mountains beyond the balcony. Here’s how to pick yours.

## Spring (March – May): blossoms and birdsong

Spring is when the valley wakes up. Almond, cherry and apricot trees flower in soft pinks and whites — including the trees in our own gardens — and the meadows turn a vivid green.

- **Weather:** mild days and crisp evenings. Pack a light jacket.
- **Why go:** blossom season, fresh mountain air and fewer crowds than peak summer.
- **Don’t miss:** a slow breakfast on the lawn while the orchard is in bloom.

## Summer (June – August): the great outdoors

Summer brings long, pleasant days — a welcome escape when the plains are sweltering. This is prime time for exploring Sonmarg, riverside picnics along the Sindh and treks into the surrounding mountains.

- **Weather:** warm afternoons, cool nights. Evenings by the bonfire are still a treat.
- **Why go:** the best season for trekking, rafting and day trips.
- **Tip:** summer is the busiest season in Kashmir, so reserve early — especially if you want the whole villa for a family or group.

## Autumn (September – November): the golden season

Many regulars will tell you autumn is Kashmir’s most beautiful season. The chinar trees turn fiery red and amber, the light goes golden, and the air sharpens. Walnut and apple harvests fill the orchards.

- **Weather:** clear skies, cool days and cold nights.
- **Why go:** spectacular colour, calm roads and perfect photography light.
- **Don’t miss:** a bonfire and BBQ evening under a sky full of stars.

## Winter (December – February): snow and stillness

Winter wraps the valley in snow. It’s quieter, slower and magical — think steaming cups of kahwa, warm blankets and snow-capped peaks at sunrise. Sonmarg becomes a wonderland, though access depends on road conditions.

- **Weather:** cold, with snowfall likely. Bring proper winter layers.
- **Why go:** snowscapes, cozy evenings and a truly peaceful retreat.
- **Tip:** check road and weather updates before travelling and keep your plans flexible.

## So, when should you come?

- **For blossoms and gentle weather:** April.
- **For trekking and adventure:** June to August.
- **For colour and photography:** October.
- **For snow and solitude:** January.
