Mountain weather changes quickly, and the temperature difference between a sunny afternoon and a starry night can be dramatic. Packing smart means you’ll be comfortable whether you’re trekking to Naranag or sitting by the bonfire.

## The essentials (all year)

- **Layers.** A base layer, a warm mid-layer and a windproof outer jacket cover almost every situation.
- **Comfortable walking shoes** with good grip.
- **Sunglasses, sunscreen and lip balm** — the mountain sun is strong.
- **A reusable water bottle.** We have a water purifier at the villa for refills.
- **Personal medication** and a small first-aid kit.
- **ID documents** and printed or offline copies of your bookings.
- **Power bank.** Handy on long day trips.

## Spring and autumn

- A warm fleece or light down jacket for the evenings
- Full-length trousers and long-sleeved tops
- A light rain jacket

## Summer

- Breathable clothing for the day
- A sweater or jacket — nights are still cool
- A hat and extra sunscreen
- Trekking gear if you plan high-altitude walks

## Winter

- Thermal inner wear
- A heavy down or insulated jacket
- Woollen cap, gloves and thick socks
- Waterproof, insulated boots for snow
