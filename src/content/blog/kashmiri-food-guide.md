Food is one of the great joys of travelling in Kashmir. The cuisine is rich, aromatic and deeply tied to hospitality — and there’s no better way to experience it than slowly, at a long table with friends and family.

## Kahwa

A fragrant green tea brewed with saffron, cardamom and cinnamon, often served with crushed almonds. It’s the drink of welcome in Kashmir — and the perfect companion to a chilly evening on the balcony.

## Noon chai

Also called sheer chai, this pink, salted tea is a Kashmiri morning staple, traditionally enjoyed with local breads from the baker.

## Kashmiri breads

Look out for the round, crisp **girda**, the sesame-topped **kulcha**, and the sweet, soft **bakarkhani** — all best eaten fresh with butter and tea.

## The Wazwan

Kashmir’s famous multi-course feast is a celebration in itself. Signature dishes include:

- **Rogan josh** — tender meat in a deep red, aromatic gravy
- **Yakhni** — a delicate, yoghurt-based curry scented with fennel
- **Rista** — meatballs in a vibrant red gravy
- **Gushtaba** — silky meatballs in a creamy yoghurt sauce, traditionally served last

## Vegetarian favourites

- **Dum aloo** — baby potatoes slow-cooked in a spiced gravy
- **Nadru yakhni** — lotus stem in a yoghurt sauce
- **Haak** — simple, comforting Kashmiri collard greens
