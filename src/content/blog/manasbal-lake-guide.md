About 23 km from Khwab Villa, Manasbal Lake is one of the valley’s calmer, less crowded lakes — a lovely contrast to the bustle of Srinagar’s waterfronts.

## Why visit

Manasbal is known for its unusually clear water and for the lotus flowers that carpet its edges in summer. Surrounded by lush greenery and hills, it’s a favourite spot for birdwatchers, photographers and anyone who simply wants to sit by the water.

## Things to do

- **Take a shikara ride.** Drift slowly across the lake and along the lotus beds.
- **Birdwatching.** The lake and its reed beds attract a variety of water birds — bring binoculars.
- **Photography.** Early morning, when the water is still and the light is soft, is the best time.
- **Picnic by the shore.** Ask us to pack snacks and a flask of kahwa for the trip.

## When to go

Summer, roughly July and August, is the best time to see the lotus in bloom. Spring and autumn bring pleasant weather and fewer visitors.

## Make a day of it

Pair Manasbal with a visit to the Kheer Bhawani Temple at Tulmulla (around 18 km from the villa) for a relaxed day of culture and nature, then head back to Khwab Villa for a bonfire under the stars.
