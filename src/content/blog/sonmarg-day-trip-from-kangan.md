Sonmarg — literally the “Meadow of Gold” — is one of Kashmir’s most celebrated destinations, and at roughly 48 km from Khwab Villa it makes an ideal day trip. The road follows the Sindh River upstream, so the journey itself is half the pleasure.

## Getting there

From Kangan, the drive continues up the Sindh Valley towards Sonmarg. Plan for a relaxed drive with photo stops — the river, pine forests and first glimpses of snow-capped peaks all compete for your attention.

**Our tip:** leave after an early breakfast. You’ll have the meadows at their quietest and the best light for photographs.

## What to do in Sonmarg

- **Walk the meadows.** Wide, open grasslands ringed by peaks — perfect for simply wandering.
- **Visit the glacier.** Thajiwas Glacier is a popular excursion, usually reached on foot or by pony.
- **Horseback riding.** Local pony operators offer rides to viewpoints and the glacier area.
- **Trekking and camping.** Sonmarg is a base for several classic high-altitude treks in summer.
- **Winter snow.** In the colder months the valley turns into a snowfield ideal for snow play, and conditions permitting, skiing and snowboarding.

## What to pack

- Warm layers — it’s noticeably cooler than Kangan, even in summer
- Sturdy, comfortable shoes
- Sunglasses and sunscreen (the sun is strong at altitude)
- Water and snacks
- A fully charged phone or camera

## Back to the villa

After a day in the mountains, there’s nothing better than returning to a warm room, a hot shower and a bonfire on the lawn. Ask us to arrange a BBQ dinner for your return.
