Just under 19 km from Khwab Villa, the Naranag temple complex is one of the most atmospheric heritage sites in the Kashmir Valley. Built around the 8th century and dedicated to Lord Shiva, its stone-carved ruins sit in a quiet valley surrounded by forests, meadows and mountains.

## What you’ll see

The complex is a group of stone temples in the classical Kashmiri style — look for the pyramidal roofs, trefoil arches and carefully fitted stone blocks that show the architectural skill of the period. Even in ruin, the setting is breathtaking.

## For trekkers

Naranag is also the starting point for some of Kashmir’s most famous high-altitude treks, including the route up to Gangbal Lake beneath Mount Harmukh. If you’re planning a trek, Khwab Villa makes a comfortable base before and after — and there’s no better recovery than a hot shower and a hearty meal.

**Note:** high-altitude treks require proper fitness, gear and a registered local guide. Check seasonal conditions before you go.

## Tips for your visit

- Go in the morning for soft light and a peaceful atmosphere.
- Wear comfortable walking shoes; the approach and grounds can be uneven.
- Respect the site — it is protected heritage, so avoid climbing on the structures.
- Carry water and a light jacket; the valley can be cooler than you expect.

## Combine it with

Naranag pairs well with a riverside lunch along the Sindh on your way back to the villa.
